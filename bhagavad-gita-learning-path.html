The अन्वयः word order, कारकचक्रम् relations and पदपरिचयः tables here are the e-reader's own analysis, reproduced exactly as its data gives them — nothing re-tagged, reordered or corrected; words the analysis supplies but the verse omits are shown in brackets, and the speaker lines are marked as not part of that analysis. A word the analysis lists more than once keeps a row for each occurrence. Translations and word glosses are short learner renderings.
